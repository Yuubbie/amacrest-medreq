1. Operational Overview & Mission Alignment

Unlike generic hospital software, NANI ReqFlow is specifically engineered for NANI's specialized reconstructive surgery and surgical rehabilitation mission for noma patients across Nigeria.

The platform enforces 100% financial and clinical transparency, donor grant accountability (ECHO, MSF, WHO compliance), patient/caregiver transport subsidies, and automated digital signature approvals across a 5-level chain of command.

3. The 5-Level Approval Chain & Digital Signatures

Every voucher progresses through an audit-locked pipeline where each approval stamps a permanent cryptographic digital signature with Name, Role, Timestamp, and unique Reference Stamp:

Level 1 · Requester Submission
Frontline Staff Member

Nurses, drivers, hostel coordinators, and therapists fill the structured voucher. Item codes, line items, and patient rosters are auto-totalled.

Level 2 · Unit & Department Supervisor
Department Head Sign-Off

Dr. Grace Mshelia (Clinicals), Mr. Patrick Adaeze (Facility), or Mrs. Stella Eze (Admin) review case justification and click Sign & Approve.

Level 3 · Finance & Grants Controller
Mrs. Bilkisu Umar (Finance Officer)

Level 4 · Programme Coordinator
Dr. Emmanuel Bawa (Programme Coordinator)

Executive authorization across clinical, facility, and administrative domains.

Level 5 · National Coordinator
Dr. Usman Sani, FWACS (National Leadership)

Automatic escalation for capital requisitions or purchases exceeding ₦500,000.

4. Hostel Patient Transport Logistics Engine

The Hostel unit features a dedicated multi-passenger schedule:

  • Patient vs. Caregiver Dropdown: Selectable per passenger.
  • State-of-Origin Auto-Rating: Rates for Borno, Yobe, Adamawa, Kano, Sokoto, Kaduna, etc.
  • Incoming vs. Discharge: Separate rates for patient arrival vs post-surgical return home.
  • Dynamic Multi-Name Table: Accommodates 15–30+ passengers in a single voucher with real-time auto-calculation.

5. Reporting & Financial Forecasting

Accessible from the Reports tab:

  • Monthly, Quarterly & Annual Cycles: Downloadable and printable in one click.
  • Donor Grant Line Breakdown: Real-time allocation, spend, and remaining balance per donor code.
  • Predictive Run-Rate Projections: Linear forecast models for Q1–Q2 2027 budget planning.
