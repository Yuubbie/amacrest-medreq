1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This operational guide accompanies the live demonstration prototype built for AMACREST Hospital & Maternity. The platform eliminates paper memo requisitions, ends administrative delays, prevents unbudgeted expenditure leakages, and gives the Chartered Accountant and executive leadership 100% real-time spending and inventory control.

The Core Administrative Philosophy:
  • Line Manager Rule: Every request originates from and is signed off by the direct departmental HOD. Top management is protected from micro-managing routine supplies.
  • Dual Pathway Split: Moving physical consumables from Central Stores is completely separated from paying cash out of the hospital bank account.

📦 Pathway A: Material Requisition

80% of Hospital Volume. Wards pulling consumables, gloves, syringes, and gauze from Central Stores. Zero cash is touched; warehouse stock is decremented immediately.

💵 Pathway B: Cash & Purchase Requisition

20% of Hospital Volume. Emergency vehicle maintenance, generator diesel, outside vendors. Directly locks against department monthly budget codes.

3. Step-by-Step Prototype Testing Guide

1Access the Live Workstation

Open the URL in any mobile browser or laptop. Click "Launch Workstation" to enter the 1-Click Role Gateway.

2Test as Frontline Staff (Raising a Requisition)

Sign in as Malam Ibrahim (Driver) or Nurse Fatima (Ward 3). Tap "+ New Requisition". Select either Material or Cash. Pick catalog items or tap "+ Add Custom Item" if unlisted, then tap Submit.

3Test Department HOD Authorization Desk (/hod)

Switch to Dr. Eze (Pharmacy) or Matron Beatrice (Nursing). Notice inboxes are strictly siloed. For store items, tap [Approve & Send to Store]; for minor cash under ₦100k, tap [Approve Disbursement]; for capital projects over ₦500k, tap [Approve & Escalate].

4Test Central Stores & Opening Balance CSV Import (/inventory)

Open the Stores Register to see stock status and safety par-levels. Click "Download CSV Template" or "Import CSV File" to experience instant Excel batch ingestion.

5Test Finance & Chartered Accountant Control Room (/finance)

View real-time department budget progress bars (Allocated vs. Spent vs. Committed) in Naira (₦) with instant monthly spend charts.
